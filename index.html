About HeadShots

HeadShots is an arena game where players can use a photo of their face on their in-game character, battle a computer-controlled opponent, or play online against a friend.

Camera & Photos

HeadShots uses the device camera to let you capture a photo for use as your in-game character's face.

HeadShots does not require an account or sign-in.

Controls

Use the on-screen controls to move, aim, and fire your toy crossbow. Shake your device to jump.

Multiplayer

One player hosts an online match and another player joins the match. If you experience connection problems, check that both devices have an active internet connection and try creating a new match.

Privacy

HeadShots requests access to your device's camera so you can capture a photo for use as your in-game character's face.

HeadShots does not require you to create an account or sign in. HeadShots does not sell your personal information.

Online multiplayer may process technical information necessary to establish and operate multiplayer sessions.
